    Duplicate songs

    Same YouTube video, or same artist and title (ignoring “Official Video”, “Lyrics”, “feat.”, “Remastered” and so on). The oldest copy is ticked as the one to keep.

    Copy to earphones

    Plug in the earphones. They show up as a USB drive (e.g. E:\ on Windows, /Volumes/SWIM on Mac). Folders that changed are copied in playlist order, because the earphones play songs in the order they were copied. Mac “._” junk files are cleaned up.

    Settings

    Deleted songs and folders go to .trash inside the library until you empty it.
