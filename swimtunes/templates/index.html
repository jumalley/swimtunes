Folders

    Download

    On YouTube or Spotify, click Share → Copy link, then paste it here. Playlists work too.

    All songs

    #TitleArtistFolderLengthSizeMove to

    Duplicate songs

    Same YouTube video, or same artist and title (ignoring “Official Video”, “Lyrics”, “feat.”, “Remastered” and so on). The oldest copy is ticked as the one to keep.

    Copy to earphones

    1. Connect the earphones to your computer with their charging cable.
    2. Wait a few seconds, then pick them below and click Copy to earphones.
    3. When it says Sync finished, eject the earphones before unplugging them.

    Your earphones

    Not in the list? Type the location yourself

    Only folders that changed are copied. Songs are copied in playlist order, because the earphones play them in the order they were copied.

    Settings

    Deleted songs and folders go to .trash inside the music folder until you empty it.

    SwimTunes is closed

    To use it again, open the SwimTunes app. You can close this tab.
